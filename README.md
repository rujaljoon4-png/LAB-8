This repository contains my Day 8 practical laboratory work for the Generative AI for Business course as a BBA Aviation Management student. The lab focuses on using Generative AI for workplace summarisation while maintaining accuracy, source fidelity and responsible human verification.

The activity introduces the concept of summary fidelity, which means representing the original source accurately without adding facts, changing meaning, omitting critical information, exaggerating conclusions, inventing statistics or presenting assumptions as facts. The lab uses an airport passenger-service report to generate an AI summary and then perform a claim-by-claim and numerical-information verification process.

The project documents the identification of omissions, distortions and unsupported information, followed by targeted revision of the AI-generated summary. It also compares the initial and corrected summaries to evaluate accuracy, completeness, limitations and suitability for management review.

A major part of the lab is creating a structured management-report scaffold with sections such as Executive Summary, Key Findings, Actions Taken, Limitations, Recommendations and Conclusion. `[VERIFY]` placeholders are used wherever additional evidence, figures or approval are required. The lab also includes a baggage-complaint summary exercise, a custom summarisation prompt, peer review and reflection.

Overall, this work demonstrates how Generative AI can support aviation reporting while requiring human verification, source checking and careful separation of facts, structure, interpretation and recommendations.
